The through-line

Learn one, know them all.

The products share a job: get out of the way of the people doing the work. That shows up as an experience you only have to learn once.

Nothing to relearn

The same layouts, the same controls, the same words for the same things. Opening your second DevCanopy product should feel like your fifth week in the first one.

Built by engineers

Every product started as something its author needed on a real team. The decisions get made by someone who has to live with them on Monday.

Narrow on purpose

Each one answers a single question and stops. Nothing here is trying to grow into a platform you have to adopt rather than just use.

The approach

Software should feel considered.

DevCanopy is independent by design: patient enough to get the details right, ambitious enough to keep growing.
